Test sounds

Open receiver.html on the phone. Set this computer's volume to about half and hold the phone 30–60 cm from the speakers.

On the phone, pick the test and tap Listen, then click a sound here. Solid buttons should pass. Dashed buttons are deliberate mistakes and should miss.

1. Ready hum (about 2.5 s)

2. Echo claps (two, a pause, three)

3. Instrument note (one note, left to ring)

4. High or low (run test 1 with the middle voice first)

Living-room noise

Leave it running and repeat the tests above.
